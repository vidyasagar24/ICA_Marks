Spreadsheet Import Guidelines
  1. Step 1: Click Download Pre-filled Template to get the official spreadsheet populated with your enrolled students' register numbers and names.
  2. Step 2: Fill marks in the respective assessment columns.
    • Enter numeric scores between 0 and maximum allowed marks.
    • Type AB if a student was Absent.
    • Type EX if a student is Exempted.
    • Leave blank if the assessment has not been conducted yet.
  3. Step 3: Do not alter the Register Number column or delete rows, as they map directly to university student records.
  4. Step 4: Upload the saved spreadsheet here. The system validates all marks, detects anomalies, and saves valid entries directly to your Draft.
